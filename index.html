Draw it in Affinity.
Get it in Studio.

Kiln turns your Affinity layers into native Roblox UI: real Frames, TextLabels and ImageButtons, with UICorner, UIStroke and UIGradient where the design calls for them. Not a flat screenshot.

Download for Windows Get started

Windows 10 and 11 · Affinity 3.3 · First release October 31, 2026

Where each part happens

In Affinity

You keep drawing as you always do. Affinity stays the source of truth for the design.

  • Kiln links to Affinity's built-in MCP server by itself
  • Every edit from Kiln is one Affinity undo step

In Kiln

Your layers show up as the Roblox tree they will become, live, next to a canvas of the screen.

  • Set classes, names and Roblox-only properties without typing tags
  • Problems like untagged or oversized images are listed before you export
  • Hover, Press, Entry and Exit animations play in Live mode

In Studio

Export, and the Kiln plugin builds the UI into StarterGui in one undo step.

  • Exporting again updates the build in place
  • Animations play in your game with no code, through KilnAnimator

Sign in with Roblox

Your UI needs its images on Roblox. When you sign in, Kiln uploads the images you export to your own account as Image assets and puts their ids in the build. An image you've already uploaded is never uploaded again.

Kiln asks Roblox for your user id and permission to read and create your assets, nothing else. The sign-in stays in Windows Credential Manager on your computer. Sign out in Kiln, or revoke Kiln in your Roblox account settings, at any time. Privacy Policy
