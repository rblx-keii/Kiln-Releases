# Kiln Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

This policy explains what Kiln does with information. "Kiln" means the Kiln desktop app, the Kiln Roblox Studio
plugin, and the KilnAnimator runtime it installs. Kiln is made by Klyne Benedict Pascual, trading as rblx-keii
("we", "us"). Contact: official.keii@outlook.com.

## The short version

Kiln runs on your computer. We don't run servers that receive your data, and Kiln has no analytics, telemetry,
tracking or ads. We don't sell or share personal information.

## What stays on your computer

- **Settings, cache and logs:** `%APPDATA%\Kiln`.
- **Your designs and exports:** your Affinity documents and `Desktop\Kiln`.
- **Your Roblox credentials:** an Open Cloud API key you enter, or the tokens from "Sign in with Roblox", are
  stored in Windows Credential Manager on your computer.
- **Your Kiln license key:** in Windows Credential Manager too. Your plan and how many new screens you've
  imported today are kept in `%APPDATA%\Kiln`, never sent anywhere.

We never receive any of this.

## What Kiln sends, and to whom

- **Roblox, when you publish.** When you choose to publish images, Kiln uploads them to Roblox's Open Cloud API
  as you, using your API key or sign-in token. Roblox handles that data under the
  [Roblox Privacy Policy](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004630823).
- **Roblox, when you sign in.** "Sign in with Roblox" opens Roblox's own page in your browser. Kiln asks only for
  the permissions it needs: your Roblox user ID (`openid`), your username (`profile`) and permission to read
  and upload your assets (`asset:read`, `asset:write`). Kiln uses your user ID to set the owner of the images you
  publish, and shows your username so you can see which account is signed in. The tokens stay on your computer.
- **GitHub, to check for updates.** Kiln may download a small file from this site
  (rblx-keii.github.io/Kiln-Releases) to see whether a new version exists. Like any web request, GitHub can see
  your IP address; the request contains no files, account details or settings. GitHub handles it under the
  [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Polar, for purchases and license checks.** Polar (polar.sh) sells Kiln for us as the merchant of record: when
  you buy, Polar collects your email address and payment details, and handles them under the
  [Polar Privacy Policy](https://polar.sh/legal/privacy). We see your email address, what you bought and your
  order history, so we can support you and handle refunds. When you activate your key, and from time to time
  while Kiln runs, Kiln sends Polar your license key and a computer label, a one-way hash of your Windows
  machine id that can't be turned back into it, so a key works on one computer at a time. Nothing about your
  designs, exports or Roblox account is sent.
- **Affinity and Roblox Studio on your computer.** Kiln talks to Affinity and to Roblox Studio over connections
  on your own computer (localhost). Nothing leaves your computer this way.

## This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which can see your IP address like any website host. It has no cookies,
analytics or tracking. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser also contacts Google, which can see your
IP address under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Buttons that say Get Pro or
Founding take you to Polar's checkout.

## Diagnostics you choose to send

**Help > Copy Diagnostics** puts a report on your clipboard: Kiln's version, Windows version, folder paths,
connection status and recent log lines. Secrets such as your API key are removed. It only reaches us if you
paste it into an email or bug report yourself, and we use it only to fix the problem you reported.

## Your choices

- Sign out of Roblox in Kiln, or revoke Kiln's access in your Roblox account settings, at any time.
- Delete your stored API key in Kiln, or remove the "Kiln" entry from Windows Credential Manager.
- Uninstalling Kiln and deleting `%APPDATA%\Kiln` removes everything Kiln stored.

## Children

Kiln is a tool for creators and is not directed at children. Use of Roblox is subject to Roblox's own age rules.

## Why we use it

The only personal information we handle ourselves is what Polar shows us about your purchase and what you send
us. We use it to:

- **give you what you bought:** your license key, downloads and support (needed to perform our contract with
  you);
- **handle refunds, taxes and accounting** (needed to meet our legal obligations);
- **answer you when you contact us** (our legitimate interest in supporting Kiln, and yours in getting help).

We don't use it for marketing, profiling or training AI, and we don't sell it.

## How long we keep it

- **Purchases** (your email address, what you bought, payments and refunds): for as long as you have a Kiln
  license, then as long as tax and accounting law requires us to keep records of sales.
- **Messages and bug reports:** only as long as needed to answer them and fix the problem.
- **License checks:** Polar keeps your key's activations (the computer label above) while the key is active. You
  can free a computer in the customer portal at any time.

## Where it's processed

Polar, GitHub, Google and Roblox may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Each
handles it under its own privacy policy, linked above, with the safeguards the law requires for international
transfers.

## Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, to export it, or to stop
using it, as provided by the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), the GDPR and UK GDPR
where they apply, and other laws that apply to you. Email us and we'll answer within 30 days. We may need to keep
purchase records the law requires, even if you ask us to delete them. If you're in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you
can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted
in the release notes of the Kiln version they apply to.
