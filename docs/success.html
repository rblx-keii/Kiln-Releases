Order reference:

Your license key

Polar, our payment provider, emails your key to the address you used at checkout. It usually arrives within a few minutes; check spam if it doesn't.

You can also see it any time in the Polar customer portal, where you can also manage your subscription and download invoices.

A key works on one computer at a time. To move it, click Deactivate in Kiln on the old computer, or free it in the customer portal.

Get started

  1. Download Kiln.
    Download for Windows
  2. Unzip it anywhere you can write to, for example Documents\Kiln. Don't run it from inside the zip or from Program Files.
  3. Run Kiln.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info then Run anyway. This early version isn't code-signed yet.
  4. Activate: in Kiln, open Settings > License, paste your key, and click Activate.
  5. Follow the Setup checklist. It links Kiln to Affinity and Roblox Studio and ticks each step off as it happens.

How imports are counted

  • One import is one unique screen: a GUI that opens on its own, like a Shop, an Inventory or a Settings menu.
  • Syncing is free. Re-importing a screen you've already imported never counts, however much you change it.
  • A new frame is a new import. Add a Rebirth screen next to your Shop and that's one new import; the Shop stays a free sync.
  • Grouping doesn't merge screens. Three screens in one group are three imports. A HUD made of pieces pinned around the screen counts as one.

Before anything counts, Kiln shows which screens it found and which are new, and you can leave some out. The count resets every day at midnight.
